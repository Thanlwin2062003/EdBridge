Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions

Who is eligible for your free training programs?

Open to all Myanmar women, especially migrant women and mothers living abroad.

How long are your training programs?

Programs range from 4 to 8 weeks depending on the course.

Do you provide job placement assistance?

Yes! We provide comprehensive job placement support.

How can I support EdBridge as a volunteer or donor?

We welcome volunteers and donations. Contact us to discuss opportunities.

Are your programs conducted online or in-person?

Currently, all our programs are conducted online via Zoom.
