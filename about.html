About EdBridge & Our Model

Our story, mission, team, and sustainable social enterprise model

Our Story

EdBridge (for Myanmar Women) Initiative was founded by displaced Myanmar individuals who believe in equality and the power of education. Shaped by lived experience, we support women affected by conflict and displacement through practical, inclusive learning opportunities.

2025

Foundation

In 2025, EBM focuses on building strong foundations through pilot education programs, system development, and regional collaboration across ASEAN countries.

2026

First Programs

EBM launches its first Education Talk Show, introducing entrepreneurship, digital skills, networking, and English education to empower women with essential knowledge and confidence.

Future

Expansion

EBM will gradually expand its training programs, partnerships, and regional reach to support more displaced Myanmar women through sustainable, education-driven solutions.
