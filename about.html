Our Story

EdBridge (for Myanmar Women) Initiative was founded by displaced Myanmar individuals who believe in equality and the power of education.

Education can bridge gaps and create opportunities even in the most challenging circumstances.

2025

Foundation

Building strong foundations through pilot education programs and regional collaboration.

2026

First Programs

Launching Education Talk Shows to empower women with essential knowledge and confidence.

Future

Expansion

Expanding training programs and partnerships to support more women through sustainable solutions.
